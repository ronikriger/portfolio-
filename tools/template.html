Quant research · Built with a partner · 2026

Kalshi weather arbitrage

An algorithm that trades Kalshi's daily high-temperature markets. It turns National Weather Service forecasts into a probability for every temperature bracket, corrects each station's bias against its official ASOS airport readings, and bids only when its fair price beats the market's ask by a set margin.

We tested it the way a trading desk would: pass and fail rules written down in advance, then a two-week forward paper-trading run. The forecasts were well calibrated (KS test p = 0.70), but the edge did not survive fees, so the rules halted the strategy and we published a full postmortem.

The bot trades only when the edge is at least 12¢, the conservative setting from the May 2026 run. Fee per contract is the larger of 1¢ or 7% of the price. The example book prices each bracket as if traders expect the "market expects" value with σ = 2.5°F, plus a 2¢ spread. Like the bot, the two end brackets use their threshold as written, so the six probabilities can add up to slightly more than 100%.

Daily Briefing

A morning news digest that learns what I want to read. At 06:23 a GitHub Actions job pulls RSS feeds, Hacker News and CISA's list of actively exploited vulnerabilities, removes duplicates, ranks the stories and emails the digest. One tap on Up or Down records a vote.

The ranker scores each story by its similarity to ones I voted on, holds back new per-source weights until enough votes arrive, and saves a few slots for stories unlike anything rated so far.

  • 762 tests
  • Supabase

AI agents · Hackathon research

skill-lift

My SkillsBench hackathon entry: five procedural skills for AI agents, plus three meta-skills for writing, validating and safety-reviewing new ones, measured in a paired sweep across two model families.

The sharpest finding was a measurement bug. The benchmark counted zero skill uses in all 51 with-skill runs, while the agents' own trajectories showed them reading skills.

  • 102 paired runs
  • 2 model families
  • bootstrap confidence intervals
